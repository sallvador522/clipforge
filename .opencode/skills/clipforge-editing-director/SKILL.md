# ClipForge Editing Director — Development Skill

This skill is **development-time guidance for the coding agent in OpenCode**. It is
not loaded by Muse Spark and has no runtime authority. The runtime knowledge asset is
`apps/api/clipforge_api/prompts/professional_editing_director.md`; the authority on
what can be planned is the capability registry in `packages/contracts`.

## Core Rule: Knowledge ≠ Capability

You may reason with the full vocabulary of professional editing below, but you may only
plan capabilities that are `available_mock`, `available_local` or `available_live` in
`clipforge_contracts.capabilities.CAPABILITIES`.

Currently available:
- `subject.isolate` — **mock** synthetic masks (not SAM)
- `background.blur` — local FFmpeg/OpenCV
- `background.replace` — local compositing with local/mock assets (not Muse Image)
- `audio.preserve` — local
- `video.render_mp4` — local
- `output.verify` — local ffprobe

Disabled until implemented: scene cuts, trimming, transitions, keyframes, multi-track,
advanced colour grading, speed ramps, professional audio mixing, animated captions.
Planned: hosted background generation.

Never describe a mock as live inference, a local asset as generated by Muse Image, or a
disabled feature as available. If a user asks for one, explain the limitation and offer a
supported alternative.

## Cinematography

- Composition: headroom, look room, rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space.
- Framing: choose shot size that serves the message; avoid cutting joints at edges.
- Camera angle: eye level flatters; high/low angles carry status and mood.
- Subject positioning: eyes near the upper third; keep motion room in the direction of travel.
- Depth and hierarchy: separate subject from background; use blur to reduce competition,
  never to hide poor isolation.
- Lighting: observe direction, softness and colour temperature; keep replaced backgrounds
  consistent with foreground light.
- Continuity: preserve eyeline, screen direction and motion across cuts.

## Professional Editing

- Workflow: ingest → review → decide → assemble → refine → verify → export.
- Transitions: cut first; use transitions only for a story reason (not decoration).
- Shot selection: choose the take with the strongest performance and cleanest continuity.
- Rhythm: pace to speech and music; hold shots long enough to read.
- Continuity: match action, position and lighting; avoid jump cuts unless intentional.
- Storytelling: every operation should serve attention, clarity or emotion.
- Non-destructive: originals are preserved; edits are new, verified exports.

## Subject and Background

- Isolation: clean alpha at hair, ears, hands and shoulders; avoid halos and chatter.
- Mask quality: validate dimensions, frame count and index continuity; feather in the
  renderer, never in the mask loader.
- Edge refinement: small feather radius; too much looks like a glow.
- Background blur: controlled depth of field; keep the subject's face untouched.
- Background replacement: match scale, horizon/perspective and light direction.
- Lighting consistency: warm subject on warm background; avoid mixed white balance.
- Natural composition: respect headroom and safe zones after replacement.

## Colour

- White balance: neutralise unintended casts; preserve intentional warmth.
- Exposure: protect highlights; avoid clipping faces.
- Contrast: shape depth without crushing shadows.
- Saturation: skin tones natural; avoid global saturation boosts.
- Temperature: match foreground/background when compositing.
- Consistency: one look across the clip.

These are guidance for explanations and future capabilities. This build does **not**
perform colour correction; do not claim it does.

## Audio

- Preserve the original audio; never regenerate speech.
- Dialogue clarity: keep levels intelligible; avoid clipping.
- Volume consistency: aim for stable perceived loudness.
- Synchronization: audio and video durations must match after render.
- Enhancement principles: gentle EQ/compression only if implemented; currently not.

## Social Formats

- 9:16 (TikTok, Reels, Shorts): subject in the central safe zone; avoid UI overlays.
- 16:9 (YouTube): preserve look room; titles inside title-safe.
- 1:1: centre-weighted composition; avoid edge-dependent framing.
- Mobile viewing: faces large enough, contrast sufficient, captions readable.
- Safe zones: keep critical action away from platform chrome.

Current build supports source aspect only; do not promise reframing or crop variants.

## Director Behaviour Rules

1. Identify subject + intended change.
2. Check the capability registry before proposing anything.
3. Choose the smallest plan that fulfils the intent.
4. Order: isolate → background → audio → render → verify.
5. Ask one focused clarification when subject/intent is ambiguous.
6. Mark approval requirements; never auto-run flagged capabilities.
7. Explain *why* in 1–2 sentences; no chain-of-thought.
8. Never emit shell commands, file paths, URLs, FFmpeg flags or arbitrary params.
9. End every executable plan with verification.
10. If a capability is unavailable, name it and offer a supported alternative.

## Implementation Rules (coding agent)

- Contracts: `ProfessionalEditingPlan` v2 in `packages/contracts`; v1 is the execution
  contract and must not be broken. Use `to_execution_plan()` for the adapter.
- Registry is authoritative: add a capability only after the backend handler and
  verification exist; then update status and tests.
- Mock director stays deterministic and offline; scenarios A–E live in
  `packages/director`.
- Playbook changes: keep required sections valid (`apps/api/clipforge_api/playbook.py`).
- Tests must prove: unknown/disabled capabilities rejected, dependency order, approval
  requirements, clarification/unsupported outcomes, adapter correctness, no network.
- No live Meta calls while implementing or testing. No secrets in code, logs or prompts.
- Keep the total project skill count ≤10 and preserve existing skills.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Every planned operation references an available capability.

- [ ] Dependencies run earlier; render and verification present.
- [ ] Clarification plans contain zero operations and one clear question.
- [ ] Unsupported plans name unavailable capabilities and do not pretend to execute.
- [ ] Mock vs local vs live status is stated truthfully in explanations.
- [ ] Approval requirements match the registry.
- [ ] Playbook loader passes; skill inventory remains ≤10.
